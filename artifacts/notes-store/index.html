Karnataka 2nd PUC Examination Scheme & Blueprint

The annual Karnataka board examination for Science subjects follows a standardized blueprint formulated by KSEAB:

  • Part A (20 Marks): 15 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and 5 Fill-in-the-Blanks testing basic recall and conceptual definitions. Zero negative marking.
  • Part B (10 Marks): Short answer questions requiring concise 2 to 3 sentence explanations or short definitions (5 questions to answer out of 10 choices).
  • Part C (15 Marks): Analytical 3-mark questions, including reasoning questions, conceptual distinctions, and short derivations (5 questions to answer out of 9 choices).
  • Part D (25 Marks): Comprehensive 5-mark long answers, standard mathematical derivations, and numerical problem solving (5 questions to answer out of 9 to 11 choices).
  • Practical Assessment (30 Marks): Laboratory experiment execution, tabular observations, calculation with error analysis, record book, and viva voce evaluation.
